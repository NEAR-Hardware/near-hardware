# NEAR Native Token

> Use NEAR to pay transaction fees, cover on-chain storage, and secure the network through staking.

NEAR (Ⓝ) is the native token of NEAR Protocol. You can transfer it between accounts, use it to pay for network resources, and stake it to help secure the network.

***

## Transaction fees

Transactions consume **gas**, which measures the computation needed to execute their actions. The sender pays for gas in NEAR.

See [Gas and execution fees](https://docs.near.org/protocol/transactions/gas.md) for how fees are calculated.

***

## Storage staking

Accounts must maintain enough NEAR to cover the data they store on-chain. These tokens remain locked while the storage is in use; removing data releases the corresponding storage stake.

See [Storage Staking](https://docs.near.org/protocol/storage/storage-staking.md) for storage costs and requirements.

***

## Validator staking

NEAR uses proof of stake to secure the network. Token holders can delegate NEAR to validators, which process transactions and produce blocks, and receive a share of staking rewards.

See [Validators](https://docs.near.org/protocol/network/validators.md) for how validators work, or follow [Staking NEAR Tokens](https://docs.near.org/protocol/tutorials/staking-tokens.md) to delegate and withdraw tokens.

> [!TIP]
>
> Smart contracts can also define their own tokens. See [Token Standards](https://docs.near.org/protocol/network/token-standards.md) for fungible tokens, NFTs, and MultiTokens.
